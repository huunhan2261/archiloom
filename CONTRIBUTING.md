# Contributing to ArchiLoom

ArchiLoom is closed-source freeware (see [EULA.txt](EULA.txt)) — the source is not published, and modification or redistribution is not permitted. So "contributing" here means **reporting bugs and requesting features**, not sending code.

## Reporting a bug

Open an [Issue](https://github.com/huunhan2261/archiloom/issues) and include:

- Your **Revit version**.
- Which **tool** on the ArchiLoom tab you were using.
- What you expected vs. what happened. A screenshot or short screen recording helps a lot.
- Steps to reproduce, if you can reliably trigger it.

## Requesting a feature

Open an Issue describing the workflow you're trying to speed up and why the existing tools don't cover it. Concrete before/after examples ("I currently do X clicks to get Y") are the most useful.

## Language

Feel free to write issues in English or Vietnamese — both are read by the author.
