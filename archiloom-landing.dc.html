Native Revit add-in · C# / .NET 8

Detailing, family housekeeping, Checking, and Camwalk.

ArchiLoom is a Revit ribbon tab of small, sharp tools for the work nobody schedules time for: renaming families in bulk, laying out detail items and sheet guides, and auditing tags, filters and view templates before you issue drawings. Then there's Camwalk — the one tool on this ribbon that isn't a dialog: a live, game-style fly-through camera for walking your 3D views. Every dialog follows one UI standard, so you learn the pattern once.
